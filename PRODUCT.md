# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + React Router + Framer Motion + Lucide React. Plain CSS / well-structured CSS architecture. No additional UI component libraries. (User-specified in brief, not delegated.)

## Users

Corporate leaders, institutional promoters, senior decision-makers, and boards who need independent strategic advisory when facing regulatory complexity, governance disputes, stakeholder negotiations, or high-stakes institutional friction. Visitors arrive to evaluate whether CMPK Raheem is the right independent advisor for a confidential, consequential matter — this is a high-trust, low-frequency decision, not a transactional purchase.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio/advisory-practice website that establishes CMPK Raheem himself — not a firm or product — as the brand. It must communicate who he is, how he thinks, what he advises on, how he works, what he has built (Vakkeel & Associates, Indian Law School), what he writes, and how to engage him, in that order. Success is a qualified senior decision-maker requesting a private briefing or confidential consultation.

## Positioning

Independent strategic advisory operating at the intersection of Strategic Advisory, Corporate Enterprise, Governance, Regulatory Intelligence, Dispute Resolution, Institutional Strategy, and Leadership Counsel. Explicitly NOT a law firm, not a political lobbying operation, not a generic consulting agency, not a SaaS product. The differentiator is independence and systemic/cross-disciplinary thinking (seeing the whole system — capital, regulation, administration, stakeholders — before recommending action) rather than narrow legal or lobbying representation.

## Operating Context

Engagements are confidential, advisory, and independent — coordinated alongside qualified legal practitioners for matters requiring formal judicial/statutory action, but CMPK Raheem Advisory itself does not claim to provide legal representation. Primary calls to action: "Request a Private Briefing" (periodic strategic briefing subscription) and "Request a Private Consultation" (confidential enquiry form). Institutions he founded/leads (Vakkeel & Associates — legal practice; Indian Law School — legal education initiative) are presented as separate chapters of his professional journey, not the same legal entity as the advisory practice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five named practice areas, each needing its own detail page (Overview, Typical challenges, Advisory approach, Relevant stakeholders, Expected engagement format): Strategic Settlement, Regulatory Intelligence, Stakeholder Negotiation, PPP Resilience, Leadership Counsel.
- No real photographs are available — hero portrait, working portrait, and institution imagery must use clearly marked placeholders ([PORTRAIT IMAGE], [WORKING PORTRAIT], [INSTITUTION IMAGE]), not generic stock photography or invented likeness.
- No CMS/backend specified — forms (briefing request, contact) are front-end only for this build; no submission backend confirmed.
- Language: British/Indian English throughout.
- Undecided: whether real published articles/insights exist (brief says to treat Insights section as clearly-labeled placeholder editorial topics, not fabricated real publications).
- Undecided: real submission handling for the two forms (briefing access, consultation) — out of scope until a backend/service is chosen.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: CMPK RAHEEM. Title: Strategic Advisor · Founder · Institutional Counsel.
- Tagline: "Where Enterprise, Governance & Leadership Intersect."
- Founded/led institutions, presented as distinct from the personal advisory brand: Vakkeel & Associates (Founder & Managing Director) and Indian Law School (Founder).
- Contact email: advisory@cmpkraheem.com.
- Strict content-integrity constraints (explicit in brief): never fabricate clients, case studies, awards, credentials, legal qualifications, government relationships, settlements, financial results, years of experience, or publications; never claim "absolute confidentiality," "guaranteed" outcomes, political access/influence, or government relationships; avoid superlative marketing language ("world-class," "India's leading," "unparalleled," etc.).

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real photographs, no verified case studies, no verified publications, no verified credentials beyond the two named founder roles stated in the brief. All portrait/institution imagery ships as explicit placeholders. Insight article topics are placeholder editorial themes only.

## Product Principles

1. The person is the brand — every section should deepen a single coherent picture of CMPK Raheem, not read as a firm/agency site.
2. Restraint signals authority — institutional, editorial, minimal; expensive because it is restrained, not because it is decorated.
3. Independence and integrity are load-bearing claims — content must stay within legally defensible, unembellished language at all times.
4. Systemic thinking over adversarial escalation — practice content should consistently frame his method as understanding the system before acting on it, never as leverage, influence-selling, or litigation-first.
5. Conceptual, not fabricated — where real evidence doesn't exist (case studies, publications), present clearly-labeled conceptual/placeholder content rather than inventing proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast against the dark editorial palette, alt text on all imagery/placeholders, `prefers-reduced-motion` support throughout, proper heading hierarchy, accessible mobile navigation (WCAG-aligned, no explicit higher standard confirmed).
